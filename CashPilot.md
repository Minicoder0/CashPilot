# AI Financial Co-Pilot — Product Backlog (Initiation)

## Feature 1 — Financial Data Intake
**Description:** Provide a reliable way for the system to obtain transaction data for analysis.

### Epic 1.1 — Provide Transaction Data
**Description:** Enable users to supply financial transaction data to the system safely and easily.

#### Story 1.1.1 — Load Demo Transaction Dataset
Allow users to instantly load a pre-prepared sample dataset to ensure the system works without requiring uploads.

#### Story 1.1.2 — Upload Transaction CSV File
Allow users to upload their own transaction file for personalized financial analysis.

### Epic 1.2 — Data Validation & Normalization
**Description:** Ensure transaction data is clean, consistent, and ready for accurate analysis.

#### Story 1.2.1 — Validate Transaction Data Format
Verify that all required fields exist and contain usable values before analysis.

#### Story 1.2.2 — Normalize Transaction Data
Standardize transaction data into a consistent format for reliable processing and analysis.

---

## Feature 2 — Transaction Intelligence
**Description:** Transform raw transaction data into meaningful financial categories and structure.

### Epic 2.1 — Categorize Transactions
**Description:** Automatically classify transactions into understandable spending and income categories.

#### Story 2.1.1 — Automatic Expense Categorization
Assign expense transactions to predefined categories to reveal spending patterns.

#### Story 2.1.2 — Income Classification
Identify and classify income transactions to clearly separate revenue streams from expenses.

### Epic 2.2 — Transaction Overview Preparation
**Description:** Prepare summarized transaction data to support financial analysis and reporting.

#### Story 2.2.1 — Generate Transaction Summary
Create a high-level summary of total income, total expenses, and transaction counts.

#### Story 2.2.2 — Identify Top Expense Categories
Determine which categories contribute most to overall expenses.

---

## Feature 3 — Financial Analysis
**Description:** Evaluate financial data to determine business health and sustainability.

### Epic 3.1 — Cashflow Analysis
**Description:** Analyze income and expenses over time to assess financial stability.

#### Story 3.1.1 — Calculate Monthly Cashflow
Compute monthly income, expenses, and net cashflow to show financial performance trends.

#### Story 3.1.2 — Calculate Burn Rate
Determine how quickly the business is spending money relative to income.

### Epic 3.2 — Financial Risk Indicators
**Description:** Identify potential financial risks that could impact business stability.

#### Story 3.2.1 — Detect Cash Shortage Risk
Assess whether the business may run out of funds based on current financial trends.

#### Story 3.2.2 — Detect Unusual Spending Patterns
Identify spikes or anomalies in expenses that deviate from normal spending behavior.

---

## Feature 4 — AI Financial Advisor
**Description:** Provide intelligent insights and guidance based on financial analysis.

### Epic 4.1 — Generate Financial Insights
**Description:** Analyze financial data to produce clear, understandable insights about business performance.

#### Story 4.1.1 — Generate AI Financial Insights
Produce natural-language insights explaining financial health, trends, and risks.

#### Story 4.1.2 — Highlight Key Financial Issues
Identify and present the most critical financial problems requiring attention.

### Epic 4.2 — Actionable Recommendations
**Description:** Deliver practical suggestions to improve financial health and stability.

#### Story 4.2.1 — Generate Cost Reduction Suggestions
Recommend ways to reduce expenses based on spending patterns and risk indicators.

#### Story 4.2.2 — Recommend Financial Actions
Provide strategic actions the business can take to improve financial outcomes.

**Description:** Present financial analysis, insights, and recommendations in a clear and visual format.

### Epic 5.1 — Financial Overview Dashboard

#### Story 5.1.1 — Display Financial Summary Cards
